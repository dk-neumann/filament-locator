How to map

  1. Click Test to confirm the page can reach WLED.
  2. Pick a rack tab. Check its first LED, where its strip starts, and which way the first shelf runs, then click Apply layout guess.
  3. For each shelf, step the cursor to the LED at the left end of the usable shelf and press L = cursor; do the same for the right end with R = cursor.
  4. The 5 slots are spaced evenly between those edges. Click Light shelf to check them (1 red, 2 green, 3 blue, 4 amber, 5 purple).
  5. Adjust the default width until slots are the right size, or fine-tune individual slots in Selected slot.
  6. Click Light all slots for a final check, then Export.
